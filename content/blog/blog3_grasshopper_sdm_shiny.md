title: Grasshopper Species Distribution Models (SDM)

An interactive web application developed as part of my PhD research, showcasing species distribution models for Western Australian grasshoppers. Users can explore habitat suitability maps, model performance metrics, variable importance, and environmental response curves for individual species.

🔗 Explore the app: https://anwarresearch-grasshopper-sdm-shiny.share.connect.posit.cloud
