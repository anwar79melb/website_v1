title: Bangladesh's Education System: Crisis, Confusion, and Loss of Direction

Bangladesh's Education System: Crisis, Confusion, and Loss of Direction is a three-part analytical series that examines the structural challenges facing Bangladesh's education sector. Drawing on historical context, policy developments, curriculum reforms, and governance issues, the articles explore how frequent policy shifts, declining educational standards, assessment-related uncertainties, and the disconnect between education and national development goals have created a crisis of direction. The series argues for a coherent, evidence-based, and future-oriented education framework that prioritizes quality, accountability, critical thinking, and the long-term needs of students and the nation.
